# Free, accountless Syncer creation remains available

Now that Accounts and paid persistence exist, we could have required an Account for every Syncer. We chose to keep the original frictionless path: a Syncer can still be created anonymously, with no Account, persisting 48 hours and using only its own Host password, exactly as before. Accounts are additive — needed only to own multiple Syncers, unlock paid persistence, or claim an existing Free Syncer after the fact — not a gate in front of the core product. This preserves the zero-friction entry point that the V1 product deliberately optimized for, at the cost of running two product tiers (anonymous and account-based) side by side indefinitely.
